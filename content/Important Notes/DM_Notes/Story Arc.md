Players feel a dark presence for the first act. Things are starting to change. [[The Panel]] falling was caused by the good guys. [[The BBEG]] is the dictator of the largest governmental power in the solar system. The [[Bandit Attack]] was carried out by his men. He didn't order it, it was on them.

The panel fell because the good guys needed a way to direct the people on the planet to a recently exposed laboratory in Russia. The town guard is sending communications to the government via the city [[Iyagi]] on the coast. [[Kenneth Tojan]] is bringing the letter to them by hand, and his wagon is the one burned out by the [[Modern-looking Bandits]] in the [[Bandit Attack]]. Kenneth is captured, and the bandits have sent a message to the BBEG. The BBEG sent a group of bandits to guard the lab, since they were stationed nearby already.
