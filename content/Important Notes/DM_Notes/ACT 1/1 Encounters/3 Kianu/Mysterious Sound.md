The players are close enough to [[Kianu]] that they can hear the church bells ring for noon.

They hear a loud *"POP"* echo throughout the forest on both sides. It is similar to the sound of a tree cracking, but not quite.

They learn from the locals that the sound has happened repeatedly over the last couple weeks, and they haven't had success in finding the source.

The players can go to the castle for information after an NPC ([[Monika Eastus]]) asks them to help because it is scaring her child ([[Rufus Eastus]]).
