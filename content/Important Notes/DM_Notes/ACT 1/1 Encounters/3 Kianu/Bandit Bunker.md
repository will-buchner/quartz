The players follow a map, given to them by the general ([[Kianu General]]) of the [[Kianu]] town guard, that has an area they must search for the bunker.

When they find it, there is one bandit inside skinning a rabbit in a table in the far back.

He can be sneak attacked, but if they don't sneak, he rolls a disadvantaged perception check.

If the players don't kill him, and instead capture him, he grinds his teeth and breaks open a fake tooth containing a lethal dose of cyanide. He says nothing before he dies.

Another bandit drops in from a hatch in the ceiling, wielding a [[Semi-Automatic Pistol]].

If he is killed, the gun can be taken by a member of the party. If he is captured, he attempts to shoot himself. If he can't, he does the same as his buddy.
