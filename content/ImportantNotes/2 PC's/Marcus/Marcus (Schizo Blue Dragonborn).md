Dillon's Character

He is afflicted with [[Schizophrenia]]. Unfortunately, he is really good at deceiving people, so when he tries to convince someone that what he saw was real, it sometimes works. When it does work, it is canonically because he unintentionally cast the cantrip Minor Illusion, and makes it seem like what he saw happened. He has Schizophrenia Medication, though.

He believes that [[The Panel]] is a giant worms egg. He found a "Book," which is actually a [[Rock with a Smashed Worm On It]].
